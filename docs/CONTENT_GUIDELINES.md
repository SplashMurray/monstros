# Content guidelines: staying original

Monstros copies **game mechanics and structure**, which copyright does not protect. It
must not copy **creative expression**. These rules apply to every contribution.
(This is practical guidance, not legal advice.)

## Allowed

- Mechanics: formulas, type matchups, stat systems, catching, evolution by level or item.
- Structure: starter choice, rival, badges, a villain team, an elite final challenge, post-game.
- Generic words as names where they are ordinary English (e.g. "Water" as a type).

## Not allowed

- Any asset, code or data taken from the original ROMs or from decompilation projects
  (e.g. `pret/pokefirered`). Do not read decompiled code to port it.
- Sprites, tiles, fonts, music or sound effects from the originals, including traced, recoloured or remixed versions.
- Monster, character, move, item, place or ability names from the originals, or near-copies of them.
- Monster designs that are recognisably based on a specific original monster.
- Map layouts that reproduce specific original towns or routes tile for tile.
- Original dialogue or signature lines. Write our own (e.g. "It's very effective!").
- Trademarks in the game, store pages or marketing ("Pokémon", "Poké Ball", "Pokédex",
  "a FireRed remake"). The capture item is a **capture orb**. The encyclopedia name is still to be decided.
- Base stat spreads copied from specific original monsters.

## Before adding a monster

1. Check that its name doesn't match or closely resemble an existing franchise monster.
2. Check that its design starts from its own idea, not from redrawing a specific original monster.
3. Write its stats from scratch: decide the role first (fast attacker, wall, ...), then pick the numbers.
